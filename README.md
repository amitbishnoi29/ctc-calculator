# CTC Calculator - Comprehensive Salary & Tax Planning Tools

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live%20Demo-ctccalculator.com-blue)](https://ctccalculator.com)

A comprehensive web application for calculating Cost to Company (CTC), tax planning, and financial analysis. This tool helps employees and employers understand salary structures, tax implications, and optimize their financial planning.

## 🌟 Features

### Salary Calculator
- Calculate CTC breakdown (Basic, HRA, DA, LTA, etc.)
- Compare old vs new tax regime
- Detailed tax calculations
- Professional tax calculations
- EPF calculations
- HRA exemption calculations

### Tax Planning Tools
- Tax Saving Investment Calculator
- Tax Bracket Calculator
- Tax Refund Estimator
- Tax Liability Projection
- Tax Optimization Tool

### Additional Calculators
- Car Loan Calculator
- Home Loan Calculator
- Personal Loan Calculator
- Education Loan Calculator

## 🚀 How to Use

1. **Salary Calculation**
   - Enter your CTC or basic salary
   - Select tax regime (old/new)
   - Add deductions (EPF, HRA, etc.)
   - View detailed breakdown

2. **Tax Planning**
   - Calculate potential tax savings
   - Compare different investment options
   - Plan your tax-saving investments
   - Estimate tax refunds

3. **Loan Calculators**
   - Calculate EMI for various loans
   - View payment schedules
   - Compare different loan options
   - Plan your repayments
